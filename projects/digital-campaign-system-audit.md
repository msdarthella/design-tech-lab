# Digital Campaign System Audit

A sample audit framework for reviewing digital campaign systems across web, email, and e-commerce touchpoints.

## Project overview

Digital campaign systems often include many repeated assets: landing pages, promotional emails, banners, product modules, social graphics, and seasonal campaign templates.

When these assets are created without shared patterns or documentation, teams can lose time recreating similar layouts, resolving inconsistencies, or making one-off decisions under tight deadlines.

This project documents how I would approach auditing a digital campaign system for consistency, accessibility, and production efficiency.

## Audit goals

- Identify repeated design patterns across campaign assets
- Find opportunities to standardize reusable templates and modules
- Improve consistency across web, email, and e-commerce experiences
- Reduce production friction for creative and marketing teams
- Support accessibility-minded design decisions

## Scope

A campaign system audit could include:

- Landing page templates
- Promotional email templates
- Hero banners
- Product feature modules
- Category or collection pages
- Paid media or social campaign assets
- Seasonal campaign variations

## What I would review

### Brand consistency

- Are typography, color, imagery, spacing, and layout choices consistent across channels?
- Do campaign assets feel connected to the larger brand system?
- Are there unnecessary one-off styles that could become reusable patterns?

### Component and template reuse

- Which modules are repeated most often?
- Are common layouts documented clearly?
- Could email, landing page, and e-commerce modules share a more consistent structure?
- Where are teams rebuilding similar assets from scratch?

### Accessibility considerations

- Is there enough color contrast between text and background?
- Are font sizes and hierarchy clear?
- Are links and calls to action easy to identify?
- Are images and visual treatments supporting the message clearly?

### Workflow friction

- Where does production slow down?
- Are handoffs clear between design, marketing, copy, and development?
- Are there missing templates, naming conventions, or usage guidelines?
- Are approvals happening late because expectations are unclear?

## Potential deliverables

- Campaign template inventory
- Reusable module list
- Accessibility notes
- Brand consistency recommendations
- Figma component or template recommendations
- Documentation for common campaign patterns
- Workflow improvement notes

## Example recommendations

- Create standardized hero, product grid, and promotional banner modules
- Document campaign layout rules for web and email
- Build reusable Figma components for common marketing patterns
- Define accessibility checks for color contrast, hierarchy, and CTA clarity
- Create a shared naming convention for campaign files and templates

## Reflection

A strong digital campaign system is not just about making assets look consistent. It is also about helping teams produce better work with less friction.

Reusable templates, clear documentation, and accessibility-minded design decisions can help creative and marketing teams move faster while protecting brand quality.
