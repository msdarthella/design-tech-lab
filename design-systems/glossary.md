# Design Systems Glossary

A working glossary of design systems terms from a visual and brand design perspective.

## Accessibility

Designing digital experiences so they can be used by as many people as possible, including people with visual, motor, cognitive, or assistive technology needs.

## Brand standards

The visual, verbal, and experiential rules that help a brand feel consistent across channels.

## Component

A reusable interface element, such as a button, card, form field, navigation item, or email module.

## Component library

A collection of reusable components organized so designers and teams can build consistent experiences more efficiently.

## Design token

A named design decision that can be reused across a system, such as a color, spacing value, typography style, radius, or shadow.

## Email module

A reusable section of an email template, such as a hero, product grid, promotional banner, footer, or content block.

## Pattern

A repeatable solution to a common design or user experience problem.

## Production workflow

The repeatable process a team uses to move work from request to concept, design, approval, build, launch, and iteration.

## Template

A reusable layout or structure that helps teams create consistent work faster.
